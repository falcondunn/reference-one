    Measuring…

    Reference One V4

    Impedance and response from the V4’s two channels: the earphone’s voltage or the mic, and its current. Every sweep here plays at −20 dBFS on one side.

    For each side, seat that earpiece in the coupler. Take its impedance with MODE at IMP, then its response with MODE at MEAS, without reseating it. The response is per volt, and the volts come from that impedance.

    The V4 is the system’s default output, so every app’s sound plays through it, uncapped. Make another device the default in the system’s sound settings.
    Two devices here are named USB AUDIO CODEC: the V4 and another product on the same TI chip (Windows calls the second one “2- USB AUDIO CODEC”). Unplug the other one, or make sure the input and the output chosen above are both the V4: Check refuses any other device.
